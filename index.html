About

I am an M.S./Ph.D. student at the Data & Adaptive Intelligence Systems (DAIS) Lab, Korea University, advised by Prof. Susik Yoon.

My research focuses on building structure-aware agentic retrieval systems that can understand and reason over structured information. I am interested in how AI systems can leverage the inherent structure of tables, graphs, and heterogeneous data to navigate information spaces, discover relevant evidence, and connect it to answer complex questions.

Ultimately, I aim to develop retrieval systems that go beyond simply finding relevant information—systems that can understand how information is structured, decide where to look next, and reason across sources to discover how pieces of knowledge fit together.
